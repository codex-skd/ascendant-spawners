<h1 align="center">Ascendant Spawners</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Silk-touch and modify mob spawners — the spawner module of Ascendant Equipment, for Minecraft 26.2.</strong></p>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2>✨ Overview</h2>

<p>Ascendant Spawners is a Minecraft 26.2 (NeoForge) mod, currently in early scaffold stage (<code>0.0.0-beta.1</code>). No gameplay features are implemented yet — this description will be expanded as the mod's mechanics are ported and built.</p>

<blockquote>This mod is a port of <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-spawners"><strong>ApothicSpawners</strong></a> by <em>Shadows_of_Fire</em>, ported from NeoForge 26.1.2 to NeoForge 26.2. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author. It is the spawner module of <strong>Ascendant Equipment</strong> (our port of Apotheosis), and depends on <strong>Common Toolkit</strong> (our port of Placebo).</blockquote>
